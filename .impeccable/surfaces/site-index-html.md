## Direction contract

THESIS: Judge an editing skill by reading the two sentences, not by reading its benchmark score. Refuse the usual feature-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Ink rules, newsprint, condensed masthead lettering and green editorial marks. Breathing room separates the brief from the actual words the run produced.

STORY: A writer recognizes a noisy sentence, checks what survived the edit, opens a different assignment, then takes the install command back to their agent.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim running masthead precedes the two-column work surface. On the left, the title and command; on the right, source and revision at reading size, with a cut marked in the source. On a phone the command stays before the proof.

FORM: An editor’s proof sheet. Use disclosures for other assignments: the reader chooses the piece rather than waiting for a carousel.

SIGNATURE: The crossed-out words are real deletions. The row count and both import times survive the edit, just as they do in the saved run.
