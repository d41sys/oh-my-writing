# Helpdesk One: lessons learned (workshop of 24 July 2026)

Present: Karl Osei, Nadia Haddad, Pavel Novak, Ines Duarte

## What worked
- Migrating only the last 24 months kept the data work to 38,500 tickets.
- Two extra training sessions for the South desk (agreed 19 May) removed most resistance before go-live.
- Four weeks of hypercare, 22 June to 17 July: 126 migration-related issues were logged and all but 2 were closed.

## What did not work
- The phone integration was added late through CR-03. It cost $7,000 plus a three-week delay, and it overran by $900. Ask about phone and chat channels in requirements.
- The South export was full of duplicates (2,300 removed). Check data quality before the migration window is fixed.

## Do next time
- Freeze scope before the plan is baselined.
- Sample each source system before agreeing a migration date.
