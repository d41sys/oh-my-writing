# evidence-grounded-writing eval

Answer each check pass or fail. Fix every failure before returning.

## Ledger and evidence

1. Does every factual, numeric, comparative and causal claim in the text appear as a ledger row, or in the change list if the task was a small edit?
2. Does each row point to an evidence location precise enough to open (file and line, table, page, URL with passage), or say honestly `none` or `memory`?
3. Was each piece of evidence actually opened before its row was marked supported?
4. Does every `partial` or `unsupported` row have an action (narrow, cut, ask) and, for narrow, the exact revised sentence?

## Claim strength

5. Is each claim worded at the strongest level its evidence supports (existence, narrow scope, systematic, guarantee), with the scope stated in the sentence?
6. Were hedge words replaced by a stated level and scope wherever the evidence is known, and kept only where the uncertainty is real?
7. Is every guarantee-style word (always, never, all, proven, guarantees) backed by a proof or exhaustive check, or reworded?
8. Does causal wording (causes, leads to, drives, because) appear only where the design isolates the cause, with correlational wording elsewhere?
9. Is every non-significant or negative result described as "not detected", with the limit of the study, rather than as "no effect"?

## Comparisons, numbers and statistics

10. Does every comparison name or imply the same baseline, metric, conditions and time, or state the differences?
11. Does every percentage change say its base, and is it clear whether the change is relative or in percentage points?
12. Does each important figure carry its unit, denominator and period, or a marker asking the author for it?
13. Did you recompute the arithmetic (changes, sums, ratios) rather than trusting the text?
14. Is each number that appears more than once identical everywhere, or is the difference named?
15. Is rounding free of changes to meaning, and is precision no finer than the measurement?

## Citations, quotes and attribution

16. Does every citation point to something that was read, and does it support the exact sentence it is attached to?
17. Is every reference in the output present in the supplied material or verified, with none written from memory?
18. Are secondhand sources marked as such, and preprints distinguished from published versions?
19. Do all quotation marks enclose exact source wording, with omissions and edits marked?
20. Was every "studies show"-type phrase named, narrowed, marked for the author, or cut?

## Uncertainty, stakes and preservation

21. Is each real limit stated once, beside the claim it limits, without blanket caveats?
22. Are health, legal, financial and safety statements either quoted from a supplied source or marked `[VERIFY with a qualified source: ...]`, with no confident advice from memory?
23. Did editing keep every qualifier, negation, number, name and the author's voice unless the ledger says it was the defect, and did no edit raise a claim's strength?
24. Is every gap handled with an `[ASK AUTHOR: ...]` or `[VERIFY: ...]` marker or one question, with no invented fact, number, source or experience?
25. If the text is not English, were only structural checks applied and the limits of lexical checks stated?

## Final read

26. Is the change list short and true: each line names what changed and why, with nothing claimed that was not done?
27. Would the author be able to reopen every cited location and confirm each supported row without asking you anything?
