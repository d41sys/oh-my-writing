# evidence-grounded-writing eval

Answer each check pass or fail. Fix every failure before returning.

## Ledger and evidence

1. Does every factual, numeric, comparative and causal claim in the text appear as a ledger row, or in the change list if the task was a small edit?
2. Does each row point to an evidence location precise enough to open (file and line, table, page, URL with passage), or say honestly `none` or `memory`?
3. Was each piece of evidence actually opened before its row was marked supported, and is every source you could not open named as such?
4. Does every `partial` or `unsupported` row have an action (narrow, cut, ask) and, for narrow, the exact revised sentence?
5. Does each row keep the statement class the author intended (fact, estimate, calculation, forecast, opinion, recommendation), with no estimate turned into a fact or scenario into a prediction?

## Claim strength

6. Is each claim worded at the strongest level its evidence supports (existence, narrow scope, systematic, guarantee), with the scope stated in the sentence?
7. Were hedge words replaced by a stated level and scope wherever the evidence is known, and kept (once, not stacked) only where the uncertainty is real?
8. Is every guarantee-style word (always, never, all, proven, guarantees) backed by a proof or exhaustive check, or reworded?
9. Does causal wording (causes, leads to, drives, because) appear only where the design isolates the cause, with correlational wording elsewhere?
10. Is every non-significant or negative result described as "not detected", with the limit of the study, rather than as "no effect"?
11. Are null results, conflicts between sources and failure conditions still in the text, with no limit cancelled by a superlative and no midpoint invented between conflicting figures?

## Comparisons, numbers and statistics

12. Does every comparison name or imply the same baseline, metric, conditions and time, or state the differences?
13. Does every percentage change say its base, and is it clear whether the change is relative or in percentage points?
14. Does each important figure carry its unit, denominator and period, or is the missing item stated plainly (or the figure left out of the reader's text) with the question in the reply?
15. Did you recompute the arithmetic (changes, sums, ratios) rather than trusting the text?
16. Is each number that appears more than once identical everywhere, or is the difference named?
17. Is rounding free of changes to meaning, and is precision no finer than the measurement?
18. When result files were supplied, was every number traced to its raw value (including run counts and whether a figure is best, mean or median), and does the report say what happens when the files are missing?

## Citations, quotes and attribution

19. Does every citation point to something that was read, and was each use checked for existence, matching details and support of its exact sentence?
20. Does the report list each failing citation use on its own line, by the citation as written, instead of burying it in a count?
21. Is every reference in the output present in the supplied material or verified, with none written from memory and no similar-looking real source substituted?
22. Are secondhand sources marked as such, and preprints distinguished from published versions?
23. Do all quotation marks enclose exact source wording, with omissions and edits marked?
24. Was every "studies show"-type phrase named, narrowed, marked for the author, or cut?

## Uncertainty, stakes and preservation

25. Is each real limit stated once, beside the claim it limits, without blanket caveats and without echoes in a summary, a closing line or a second section of the report?
26. Are health, legal, financial and safety statements in the prose either quoted from or attributed to a supplied source, with `[VERIFY: qualified source for ...]` kept in the ledger and reply, and no confident advice from memory?
27. Did editing keep every qualifier, negation, number, name, scope word (species, population, setting, period) and the author's voice unless the ledger says it was the defect, and did no edit raise a claim's strength?
28. Is every gap either stated plainly in the reader's text ("the sources do not report X") or left out, and logged in the ledger, reply and (for saved files) `open-questions.md` as an `[ASK AUTHOR: ...]`, `[VERIFY: ...]` or `[UNTESTED]` line, with no invented fact, number, source or experience and no other marker spelling (to-do, citation-needed or confirm tags) left anywhere?
29. If the text is not English, were only structural checks applied and the limits of lexical checks stated?

## Final read

30. Is every removed claim listed in the change list or cut-claims table, so nothing was hidden by rewording? Did the author's own measurements survive, with the overclaiming interpretation fixed rather than the figures cut?
31. Is the change list short and true: each line names what changed and why, with nothing claimed that was not done?
32. Does the report say what was checked and what was not, avoid claiming "all verified", and note that the check is not independent if you wrote the text?
33. Would the author be able to reopen every cited location and confirm each supported row without asking you anything?
34. Is the reader-facing text (revised passage, written draft) free of `[ASK AUTHOR]`, `[VERIFY]`, `[UNTESTED]` markers and of meta-notes (process narration, "as requested", per-paragraph evidence remarks, `[source: ...]` tags), with the change list, ledger and questions in the reply, and `open-questions.md` written when files were saved? If the author asked for a draft to edit, are the markers beside the gaps and is it said to be a draft? (Audit reports, ledgers and citation checks are author-facing and keep markers.)
35. Does the revised or new text read as the author wrote it (their person and register; no heading added to a text under about 400 words or to an email, letter or post; no signposting; plain transitions; prose for a few items), and does the audit report state each finding once instead of in both a ledger and a findings section?
