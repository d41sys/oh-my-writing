# independent-review eval

Answer each check pass or fail. Fix every failure before returning.

## Independence

1. Was every review round run in a new session, subagent or process, rather than in the writer's own context?
2. If no fresh context was available, does the report say "Independent review: not run" with the reason, instead of presenting a self-check as a review?
3. Where a different model family was possible, was it used, and does the report state the family (or "unknown")?
4. Does the packet sent to the reviewer contain only the artifact, the reader brief, hard constraints, sources, and an optional rubric?
5. Is the packet free of the writer's intent, hoped-for conclusion, requested verdict, drafting history, and earlier versions?
6. Is the packet free of any statement of what was fixed or changed since an earlier round?
7. Are the review questions open ("what is the main point?") rather than leading ("isn't this clear?")?
8. Are sources passed as files or raw material, not as your summary of them?

## Fresh-reader test

9. Did you write the expected main point and expected reader questions before reading the reader's reply?
10. Is the reader's restated main point compared with the brief and labelled matches, partly, or misses?
11. Is every stall classified as text defect, audience boundary, reader limitation, or out of scope, with only text defects fixed?
12. Were test questions written without containing their own answers?

## Findings and triage

13. Does every finding carry a severity, a location (quote or section), and a reader consequence?
14. Is severity assigned by reader impact rather than reviewer tone, and are taste-only items marked nit?
15. Does every finding have exactly one decision: accept, reject with a one-line evidence-based reason, or needs author?
16. Was any finding that would add an unsupported claim, number, quote, name or example rejected as written, and turned into `[ASK AUTHOR: ...]` if the gap is real?
17. Are accepted fixes the smallest edits, with the author's voice, claims, qualifiers, numbers and structure intact?
18. Were fixes applied in dependency order and diffed against the version the reviewer saw for drift?

## Calibration and comparison

19. For subjective criteria, does the report state calibration status, and say `calibration: none` rather than inventing anchors?
20. Were any good/bad anchor examples chosen by a human, and did the sanity test show the bad examples scoring below the good ones?
21. Are reviewer disagreements explained (cause and action) instead of averaged?
22. If two versions were compared, were labels anonymous, order randomized, and the comparison swapped or reported as order-sensitive?

## Stopping and reporting

23. Does the review stop within two rounds, or is the extra round approved by the user?
24. Is the "edit, re-review, repeat until clean" loop absent?
25. Are unresolved critical or major items listed under Remaining, with the reason and the effort needed?
26. Does the report say what the review does not cover (for example technical correctness, legal or medical accuracy), and route those to a specialist or the author?
27. Is the depth (light, standard, high) stated and proportionate to the stakes, with the number of sessions used?
28. Is the report short, with changes listed one line each, and no claims of verification you did not perform?

## Final read

Read the report once as the author. Does it say what to do next, what was rejected and why, and what only the author can answer? If a sentence would not change what the author does, cut it.
