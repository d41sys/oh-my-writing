## Same idea

Share of our sentences whose best sentence has embedding cosine at or above 0.75: the precise cut, which misses paraphrases that score lower. The estimate adds the sentences between 0.60 and 0.75 at the rate a hand-read uniform sample found (15 of 60 pairs stated the same rule; 95% interval for that rate 15.8% to 37.2%). The sample took each sentence's best match over all sources, so *any source* is the design-matched estimate; for one source the estimate is an upper bound. Both count advice two authors reach independently, so they measure overlap of ideas, not copying; the control rows give the noise floor. The pairs were read generously: general advice such as cutting filler counted as the same rule.

## Rule inventory

| our skill | source | source rules covered by our skill (firm / firm+likely) | covered by any of our skills | our rules that trace to the source (firm / firm+likely) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| anti-slop-editing | avoid-ai-writing (92 rules) | 68.5% / 82.6% | 77.2% / 91.3% | 62.8% / 76.9% of 78 |
| author-voice | humanizer (26 rules) | 11.5% / 23.1% | 65.4% / 88.5% | 11.9% / 28.6% of 42 |

A source rule is *covered* when one unit of our files (a section, list item or table row) has cosine at or above the cut with it or a heading there names the same thing; an *our rule traces* when the same holds in the source's files. *Firm* is the precise cut, *likely* adds the lower cut.

## Method

Corpora: for each of 17 sources, every SKILL.md and the text files in that skill's folder (references, assets, rules, scripts, agents), or, for guide and prompt collections, their content files; README, LICENSE, CHANGELOG, translations, tests, whole-file bundles and hidden files are left out, and so are non-English files. Our side is every text file in `skills/<name>/` except `eval.md`. Each file is split into prose (Markdown outside fences, comments and docstrings, YAML and JSON string values, HTML and TeX text) and code. Prose levels: *verbatim* is the share of our words covered by 8-word runs that also occur in the source; *close paraphrase* is the share of our sentences of 6+ words, not already verbatim, whose best source sentence has word-trigram Jaccard of 0.5 or more; *same idea* is the share of our sentences whose best source sentence has cosine of sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings (cosine) at or above 0.75, a cut set by reading 96 sample pairs (30 of 36 pairs at or above the cut stated the same rule); a separate uniform random sample of 60 pairs from the band just below the cut (0.60 to 0.75) gave 15 pairs that stated the same rule, which is the recall adjustment. Code: lines with comments and all whitespace removed, dropped under 20 characters, matched exactly, plus runs of 5+ consecutive kept lines. The control is the same measurement against unrelated jezweb skills (dev-tools, cloudflare, shopify, wordpress, frontend, integrations, design-assets); it sets the noise floor, since advice such as *cut filler* recurs in any writing guide and semantic overlap on writing advice is partly unavoidable. Verbatim and code-line figures are the copying measures; same-idea figures are not evidence of copying.
