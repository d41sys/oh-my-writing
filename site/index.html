results-visualization

Plot experiment results as paper-ready charts in one consistent style, read from the result files.

Plot these results for the paper

Request: plot these results for the paper

You get: PDF and PNG figures, the script or JSON spec that rebuilds them, a caption and the one-sentence finding for the text.

Two panels, IID and non-IID

Request: two panels, IID and non-IID

You get: numbers from the result files. A zoomed axis, unit mismatch or unreadable bubble size is fixed or stated in the caption.

Confusion matrices for three datasets

Request: confusion matrices for the three datasets

You get: figures drawn from your result files or results table. LaTeX text is used when latex is installed.

Restyle this figure like my notebook

Request: redo this figure in the style of my notebook

You get: the figure redrawn in the look of your plotting notebook or earlier figures.

Eleven skills side by side

SkillYou ask forYou giveYou get
anti-slop-editingAn edit, or a report of AI tells with no editsThe text, where it will appear, passages that must not changeRevised text and a list of changes. Text that is already fine stays unchanged
author-voiceA voice profile, a draft in your voice, a check of a draftThree or more samples of your writing, 150 words or more eachA profile file you can reuse, or a draft that keeps your habits
structured-draftingA draft from notes, an outline, co-writing section by sectionNotes, transcripts, source excerpts or a rough draft, and who will read itA draft. Bigger jobs get a brief and an outline first
evidence-grounded-writingA fact-check, a citation audit, toned-down claims, a claims ledgerThe text and the sources or result files behind itA claims ledger, and each claim at the strength its evidence supports
independent-reviewA review without your context, a reviewer prompt, triage of commentsThe draft, or the review commentsFindings with severity, the exact quote and the consequence, or a prompt to paste
post-writingBlog post, essay, newsletter, LinkedIn post, X thread, launch announcement, onboarding emailsThe material, the platform, the audience, a post of yours to matchA post that opens on something concrete, with no invented story or metric
report-writingDecision memo, executive summary, research or market report, vendor comparison, post-mortemNotes or dataThe conclusion first. No recommendation the material does not make
technical-docsREADME, tutorial, how-to, API or CLI reference, architecture explanation, PR description, commit message, changelog, release notes, migration guide, ADRCode, a diff, commits or a configThe page type the reader needs. Commands it did not run are marked [UNTESTED]
career-documentsRésumé, CV, cover letter, statement of purpose, LinkedIn profile, cold emailYour background and the target job ad or program pageA tailored document with nothing inflated. Missing metrics are asked for
project-reportA project report or status report from a folderA folder of docx, xlsx, pptx, pdf, eml, csv or md filesOne print-ready HTML file, a Markdown copy, a fact ledger and open questions
results-visualizationCharts for a paper, multi-panel figures, confusion matrices, a restyleResult files (CSV, JSON, logs) or the results tablePDF and PNG figures, the script that rebuilds them, a caption and the finding
